Hellou AI is the company behind HellouOne, a shared inbox for WhatsApp, web chat, email and social that answers leads in seconds with AI agents. It offers multi‑channel communication, AI voice calls, and team collaboration, with plans starting at $49/mo. The platform serves Latin America and the US, providing AI‑driven customer support and sales automation.

**Areas:** AI · Communication · Customer Support · WhatsApp · SaaS

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Hellou AI: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
